# CashQuest MVP

CashQuest is a static marketplace prototype for paid business tasks.

## Revenue model
- Business campaign/platform fees.
- Advertising placements (requires approval and real ad-network code).
- Optional business subscriptions/analytics.
- Clearly labeled affiliate offers, where appropriate.

## Current status
This repository is a **demo**, not a real-money marketplace. The wallet uses browser localStorage and must not be represented as withdrawable cash.

## Production roadmap
1. Authentication and user/business accounts.
2. Database-backed quests, submissions, balances and audit logs.
3. Business payment collection and escrow.
4. Server-side proof review and anti-fraud controls.
5. Real payout provider and required tax/KYC workflows.
6. Terms, privacy, eligibility and marketplace policies.
7. Ad-network approval and publisher configuration.
8. Analytics, moderation and abuse reporting.

Revenue is not guaranteed; it depends on users, advertisers, business customers and completed transactions.
