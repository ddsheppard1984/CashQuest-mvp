HOW IT WORKS

One marketplace. Two sides.

A company defines a task, location, proof requirements and budget.

02

Users choose eligible quests and submit the requested proof.

03

CashQuest verifies submissions and handles fraud controls before a reward becomes payable.

04

Once the payment system is live, approved rewards can be paid and CashQuest can earn platform fees.
